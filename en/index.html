The menu

Dishes with a story

Every dish is freshly prepared from family recipes handed down from generation to generation. Everything can be tailored to your event.

Jollof Rice

Our famous one-pot rice in a rich tomato sauce with chicken, beef or vegetarian. The star of every Ghanaian party.

Signature dish

Kelewele

Golden fried plantain with ginger, chilli and Ghanaian spices. Sweet, spicy and irresistible as a snack.

Perfect for receptions

Banku & Tilapia

Grilled tilapia with fermented corn dough balls and fresh pepper sauce. Tradition at its best.

Traditional

Groundnut Soup

Creamy peanut soup with tender chicken, served with fufu. Comfort food the way only grandmother makes it.

Comfort classic

Waakye

Rice and beans steamed in sorghum leaves, with shito, gari, spaghetti and egg. The street food of Accra.

Street food favourite

Chichinga

Grilled skewers marinated in suya spices. Smoky, spicy and always the first to go.
